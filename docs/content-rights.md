already embedded in [`rogue_rewards.py`](../src/launcher/rogue_rewards.py).

author, work, location and translator credits. The reviewed evidence supports
public-domain use in the United States and Brazil. It does not claim a
worldwide copyright determination or cover other translations or media.

For the United States, each official catalog record explicitly marks its book
public domain: [5682](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/5682),
[2680](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/2680) and
[1998](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/1998). The reviewed early publication
dates are consistent with the Copyright Office's
[duration guidance](https://www.copyright.gov/circs/circ15a.pdf). This decision
uses those exact old translations, not a modern translator's rendering.

For Brazil, [Law 9,610/1998, articles 41 and 45](https://www.planalto.gov.br/ccivil_03/leis/l9610.htm)
sets the economic-rights term at seventy years from 1 January after death and
includes works whose term expired in the public domain. Applying that rule
to the identified authors and translators supports expired economic rights
for these excerpts by the review date; the latest translator died in 1919.
Existing credits preserve authorship and translation identity. This reasoning
does not rely on a fair-use exception or on the original author's age alone.

## Text and trademark boundary

The [source provider's policy](https://www.gutenberg.org/policy/license.html)
distinguishes unrestricted book text from its trademark and license materials.
Its examples expressly permit linked references in acknowledgements or
reference sections. The runtime excerpts contain no Gutenberg name, logo,
license, header or footer. The links above identify evidence and do not imply
endorsement. Keep those materials out of the incorporated passages; a new
translation, modern editorial addition or complete eBook requires its own
review.

This record closes the existing translation question for the stated US/Brazil
scope. It grants no rights to gallery images, cars/tracks, album artwork,
voices, Toasty media or optional mods. Those assets retain their separate
provenance and distribution decisions in the
[integration inventory](third-party-integrations.md) and
[dependency review](dependency-license-review.md).
